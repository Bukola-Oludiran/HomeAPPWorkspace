# Tax Obligations of HomeAI (Nigerian Prop-Tech Platform) under the 2025 Tax Reform Laws, as of October 2026

Research date: 26 Oct 2026. Primary texts used: (a) the gazetted Nigeria Tax Act 2025 (NTA) as hosted by Gombe State IRS ([NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf); also at [NASS](https://nass.gov.ng/documents/download/11249)); (b) the National Assembly "Approved Copy to Print" (certified) Nigeria Tax Administration Act 2025 (NTAA), released Jan 2026 ([NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)). Section numbers below are from these copies. **Warning: several versions of the Acts are in circulation and some figures differ between them (see Q1 and Q3). Every threshold below should be checked against whichever version NRS currently applies.**

Legend: **[OLD]** = pre-2026 rule; **[PENDING]** = regulation/guidance outstanding; **[CONFLICT]** = sources disagree.

## Q1. The four 2025 Acts: effective dates and what they replaced

### Takeaway
Four Acts were signed on 26 June 2025. The NRS (Establishment) Act and the Joint Revenue Board Act took effect on signing. The NTA and NTAA apply from 1 January 2026. The NTA repeals CITA, PITA, the VAT Act, CGTA, the Stamp Duties Act and others, and the NRS replaces FIRS. A live controversy over differences between the gazetted and NASS-certified texts affects some thresholds.

### Cited Findings
- The NTA's gazette header shows commencement "[26th June, 2025]". The Act is 2025 No. 7, and the Clerk of the National Assembly certified it on 11 June 2025 — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- EY says President Tinubu signed the NTA on 26 June 2025 and that it takes effect on 1 January 2026 — [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/nigeria-tax-act-2025-has-been-signed-highlights)
- The Federal Ministry of Finance transition guidelines say the Acts apply from the commencement date in each law, which is 1 January 2026 for the NTA. Liabilities for earlier periods stay under the repealed laws — [FMINO transition guidelines](https://fmino.gov.ng/?p=52409); [Arise](https://www.arise.tv/nigeria-gazettes-tax-reform-laws-implementation-to-begin-january-2026/)
- NTA s.196 repeals, from its commencement: the Capital Gains Tax Act, Casino Act, Companies Income Tax Act, Deep Offshore and Inland Basin Act, Industrial Development (Income Tax Relief) Act (pioneer status), Income Tax (Authorised Communications) Act, Personal Income Tax Act, Petroleum Profits Tax Act, Stamp Duties Act, Taxes and Levies (Approved List for Collection) Act, Value Added Tax Act, and Venture Capital (Incentives) Act — [NTA s.196](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- The NTA's long title also amends the NITDA Act, TETFund Act, NASENI Act, Nigeria Start-up Act, Cybercrime Act and others. This is how the earmarked levies were folded into the development levy — [NTA long title](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- The NTAA (2025 No. 5) also shows commencement "[26th June, 2025]". It provides uniform procedures for administering federal and state taxes, including registration, returns, assessments, disputes and penalties — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf); [KPMG NTAA](https://kpmg.com/ng/en/home/insights/2025/06/the-nigeria-tax-administration-act-ntaa-2025.html)
- The NRS (Establishment) Act 2025 was assented to and commenced on 26 June 2025. It repeals the FIRS (Establishment) Act 2007 and rebrands FIRS as the Nigeria Revenue Service — [judy.legal](https://www.judy.legal/legislation/akn/ng/act/2025/4); [KPMG NRS Act](https://kpmg.com/ng/en/home/insights/2025/07/the-nigeria-revenue-service-establishment-act-2025.html); [LawNigeria](https://lawnigeria.com/2025/09/24/nigeria-revenue-service-establishsment-act-2025/?amp=1)
- The Joint Revenue Board of Nigeria (Establishment) Act 2025 also commenced on 26 June 2025. It replaces the Joint Tax Board framework and creates the Joint Revenue Board, the Tax Appeal Tribunal (now covering disputes under both federal and state tax laws) and the Office of the Tax Ombud — [KPMG JRB Act](https://kpmg.com/ng/en/home/insights/2025/07/the-joint-revenue-board-establishment-act-jrea-2025.html); [judy.legal](https://www.judy.legal/legislation/akn/ng/act/2025/6)
- **[CONFLICT] Versions of the text.** A House of Reps minority caucus panel said it "confirmed alterations in gazetted tax laws" and found at least three versions of the NTAA in circulation — [Daily Trust](https://dailytrust.com/just-in-weve-confirmed-alterations-in-gazetted-tax-laws-reps-panel/). The House released certified true copies in January 2026 — [AllAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202601050427.html). Commentary lists nine differences between the gazetted and NASS-certified versions, including the small-company turnover limit (₦50m in the gazette vs ₦100m in the final version). The National Assembly said it would work with the Government Printer to publish duly certified Acts. No completed re-gazetting was reported as of Jan 2026 — [Arbiterz, 9 Jan 2026](https://arbiterz.com/gazetted-vs-national-assembly-versions-why-9-key-differences-in-new-tax-law-dont-really-matter/); [BusinessDay opinion](https://businessday.ng/opinion/article/one-law-two-scripts-navigating-the-material-discrepancies-in-the-nigeria-tax-act-2025/)
- Taiwo Oyedele (Presidential Tax Reform Committee) was reported as acknowledging discrepancies, but later denied admitting errors and called some circulating gazettes fake — [TheCable](https://www.thecable.ng/despite-video-evidence-taiwo-oyedele-denies-acknowledging-errors-in-new-tax-laws/); [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/news/article/oyedele-labels-circulating-gazetted-bills-fake-amid-legislative-backlash/)

### Inferences
- In practice the regime has been operating since 1 Jan 2026 (NRS circulars dated 2026 exist; see Q3 and Q4), even though the gazette bracket shows 26 June 2025.
- HomeAI's FY2026 is the first year fully under the NTA/NTAA. Any 2025 periods fall under the repealed laws.

### Gaps
- No statutory "1 January 2026" commencement clause was found in the gazetted NTA or certified NTAA text extracted. The 1 Jan 2026 date rests on the FMoF guidelines and adviser commentary.
- It is unclear whether a corrected or re-gazetted NTA was published after Jan 2026.

## Q2. Tax ID (TIN) registration: process and authority

### Takeaway
Every taxable person must register with the "relevant tax authority" and get a Tax ID (NTAA s.4). For a company, that authority is the NRS. For individuals (including employees whose PAYE HomeAI deducts), it is the state IRS where they reside: LIRS in Lagos, FCT-IRS in Abuja. Banks must make a Tax ID a precondition for operating accounts.

### Cited Findings
- NTAA s.4: "Every taxable person shall register with the relevant tax authority and obtain a Taxpayer Identification ('Tax ID')". Under s.7 the authority must issue a Tax ID on request, may register persons who failed to apply, must give reasons within 5 working days if it refuses, and one person may hold only one Tax ID — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
- Under NTAA s.8, the Tax ID must appear on returns, correspondence and "a document … issued … in respect of a transaction" (e.g., invoices). Banks, insurers, stockbrokers and other financial service providers must make it a precondition for opening or operating any account. Under s.9, changes in particulars must be notified within 30 days — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
- NTAA s.3: the NRS administers income tax (companies), stamp duties, VAT, development levy and tax incentives. State/FCT authorities administer taxes of resident individuals (First Schedule). Under s.87, State IRSs keep their role for individuals' income tax, PAYE and stamp duties — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
- Penalty for failing to register (NTAA s.100(1)): ₦50,000 in the first month and ₦25,000 for each later month. Under s.100(2), a company that awards a contract to an unregistered person faces a ₦5,000,000 penalty — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf); [TheCable](https://www.thecable.ng/firs-to-penalise-companies-for-undocumented-transactions-undisclosed-office-address/)
- Practitioner sources say a new 13-digit system-generated Tax ID went live on 1 Jan 2026, verifiable at taxid.nrs.gov.ng (single secondary source) — [Commenda](https://www.commenda.io/blog/nigeria-tin-verification)
- A practitioner source says Tax ID / VAT registration for CAC-registered businesses is now largely automatic because the Tax ID is linked to the CAC registration number (not confirmed officially) — [Lawpadi](https://lawpadi.com/how-to-register-for-vat-in-nigeria/)
- Non-resident suppliers of taxable goods or services, or persons deriving income from Nigeria, must register (NTAA s.6). Investors with only passive income are exempt. EY flags this as a significant shift — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf); [EY Feb 2026](https://globaltaxnews.ey.com/news/2026-0447-nigeria-tax-registration-for-nonresident-companies-undergoing-subtle-but-significant-shift-in-nigerias-international-tax-landscape)

### Inferences
- HomeAI should register as an employer with LIRS (Lagos) or FCT-IRS (Abuja) for PAYE, in addition to its NRS Tax ID.
- Under s.100(2), HomeAI should collect and verify Tax IDs from agents, contractors and vendors before contracting. Engaging an unregistered agent can cost ₦5m per contract.
- Foreign SaaS vendors (cloud, maps, AI APIs) are now expected to register in Nigeria. Where they do not, HomeAI may have to self-account for VAT (see Q4).

### Gaps
- No primary NRS guidance on the Tax ID portal workflow was retrieved.
- LIRS/FCT-IRS employer-registration forms were not retrieved.

## Q3. Company income tax: rate, small-company exemption, development levy, minimum tax, filing

### Takeaway
CIT is 0% for a "small company" and 30% for other companies (NTA s.56). A small company must meet both tests: turnover ≤₦50m (gazetted NTA) or ≤₦100m (NASS-certified final text and NTAA), **[CONFLICT]**, and fixed assets ≤₦250m. Professional-service businesses never qualify. A 4% development levy (s.59) applies to non-small companies and replaces TETFund tax and the other earmarked levies. The 15% minimum effective tax rate applies only to large or MNE companies. Returns are due 6 months after year end, or within 18 months of incorporation for new companies.

### Cited Findings
- NTA s.56: tax on total profits is "(a) a small company, at 0%; and (b) any other company, at the rate of 30 per cent from the commencement of this Act" — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/nigeria-tax-act-2025-has-been-signed-highlights)
- **[PENDING]** A cut of the rate from 30% to 25% for large companies is reported to depend on a commencement date set by the President on the advice of the National Economic Council. No order was found — [Arise](https://www.arise.tv/nigeria-gazettes-tax-reform-laws-implementation-to-begin-january-2026/)
- **[CONFLICT] Small company definition.**
  - Gazetted NTA s.202: "'small company' means a company that earns gross turnover of N50,000,000 or less per annum with total fixed assets not exceeding N250,000,000, provided that any business providing professional services shall not be classified as a small company" — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/nigeria-tax-act-2025-has-been-signed-highlights)
  - The certified NTAA (s.147 interpretation) defines "Small Business" as gross turnover of "N100,000,000.00 or less per annum with total fixed assets not more than N250,000,000.00", with the same professional-services exclusion — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
  - Commentators say the final NASS-approved NTA raises the limit to ₦100m — [Arbiterz](https://arbiterz.com/gazetted-vs-national-assembly-versions-why-9-key-differences-in-new-tax-law-dont-really-matter/); [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/ng/en/services/tax/nigeria-s-tax-reform-in-focus/2025-nta-vat-key-business-changes)
  - Others maintain that ₦50m is the operative gazetted figure (published Aug 2026) — [Taxjeje](https://taxjeje.com/insights/vat-threshold-50-million-not-100-million); [Tundeadisa](https://tundeadisa.com/media_insights/know-your-taxes-the-tax-reforms-and-your-statutory-filing-obligations-a-simplified-guide/)
- **Development levy (NTA s.59).** 4% "on the assessable profits of all companies chargeable to tax … other than small companies and non-resident companies", collected by NRS. Proceeds are split: TETFund 50%, Nigerian Education Loan 15%, NITDF 8%, NASENI 8%, National Board for Technological Incubation 4%, Defence and Security Infrastructure Fund 10%, National Cybersecurity Fund 5% — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf); [KPMG Oct 2026](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/10/nigeria-corporate-income-tax-15-percent-minimum-tax.html)
- The development levy is filed with CIT. Failure to file attracts ₦100,000 in the first month and ₦50,000 for each later month — [Tundeadisa](https://tundeadisa.com/media_insights/know-your-taxes-the-tax-reforms-and-your-statutory-filing-obligations-a-simplified-guide/)
- **[CONFLICT] Minimum effective tax rate (s.57).** Where the ETR is below 15%, the company pays a top-up to 15%. It applies to constituents of MNE groups and to other companies above a turnover threshold, which is "N20,000,000,000 and above" in the gazetted text — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/nigeria-tax-act-2025-has-been-signed-highlights). NRS Information Circular 2026/01 (29 June 2026) applies the 15% minimum to MNE constituents (group turnover ≥750m, a figure KPMG reports in pounds rather than euros) and to companies with turnover of **₦50 billion** or more — [KPMG Oct 2026](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/10/nigeria-corporate-income-tax-15-percent-minimum-tax.html). ETR profits are audited PBT less 5% of depreciation and personnel costs (gazetted s.57(4)). The final version reportedly uses audited PBT only — [Arbiterz](https://arbiterz.com/gazetted-vs-national-assembly-versions-why-9-key-differences-in-new-tax-law-dont-really-matter/)
- Circular 2026/01 also covers loss rules. Losses offset only against the same trade or business and carry forward indefinitely. Digital/virtual-asset losses offset only against related gains. Capital allowances are generally claimable in full where profits suffice — [KPMG Oct 2026](https://kpmg.com/us/en/taxnewsflash/news/2026/10/nigeria-corporate-income-tax-15-percent-minimum-tax.html)
- **Filing (NTAA, company returns section; s.11 per table of contents, not verified).** Returns are due no later than 6 months after the end of the accounting year for companies in business more than 18 months. A new company files within 18 months of incorporation or 6 months after its first accounting period end, whichever is earlier. The return must include a self-assessment form, audited financial statements, tax and capital-allowance computations, evidence of payment, an ETR computation and a principal officer attestation. A small company may file accounts attested by the taxpayer instead of audited accounts — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
- NRS may extend the filing deadline by up to 2 months. Companies may apply in writing to pay in instalments, with the final instalment due by the filing deadline. Additional assessments are limited to 6 years except for fraud or wilful default — [PwC tax administration, reviewed 29 May 2026](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nigeria/corporate/tax-administration)
- Records must be kept for at least 6 years (NTAA) — [Tundeadisa](https://tundeadisa.com/media_insights/know-your-taxes-the-tax-reforms-and-your-statutory-filing-obligations-a-simplified-guide/); [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
- Companies claiming incentives must also file annual tax incentive returns (NTAA s.27) — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)

### Inferences
- An early-stage HomeAI with turnover under ₦50m (or ₦100m if that figure prevails) and fixed assets of ₦250m or less pays 0% CIT, 0% CGT and no development levy. It must still register and file annual returns, and it may use self-attested accounts.
- Turnover from commissions, listing fees and subscriptions counts toward the threshold. Rent passing through HomeAI's escrow should not count as HomeAI's turnover if it is properly structured as client money (an inference, not confirmed by any source).
- Risk: if NRS treated HomeAI's agent, brokerage or advisory services as "professional services", the small-company exemption would be lost whatever its size. "Professional services" is undefined in the extracted text, so this needs advice.
- Once above the threshold, the effective burden is 30% CIT plus 4% development levy on assessable profits. The 15% minimum ETR will not apply to an early-stage startup.

### Gaps
- Definition of "professional services" for the small-company exclusion was not found.
- **[OLD]** The pre-2026 CIT tiers (CITA small/medium/large), TET 3%, NITDA 1%, NASENI 0.25% and Police Trust Fund levies were not re-verified in this research.
- Whether NRS officially applies ₦50m or ₦100m in 2026 assessments was not established. No NRS circular on the small-company threshold was found. Circular 2026/01's use of ₦50bn for the minimum ETR (the "final version" figure) suggests NRS works from the NASS-certified text, but this is unconfirmed.

## Q4. VAT: rate, registration threshold, digital/platform services, residential rent, marketplace duties, e-invoicing

### Takeaway
VAT is 7.5% (NTA s.148). "Land or building including interest in land or building" is VAT-exempt (s.186(1)(l)), so rent, residential or commercial, carries no VAT. HomeAI's own commissions, listing fees, subscriptions and escrow or service fees are taxable services. Small businesses are exempt from VAT registration, charging and returns but may opt in. Returns are due by the 21st monthly. Fiscalisation/e-invoicing via the NRS Merchant Buyer Solution is phased, with go-live for small taxpayers (turnover under ₦1bn) in July 2027.

### Cited Findings
- NTA s.148: VAT "at the rate of 7.5%" — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- NTA s.186(1) exempt supplies include "(l) land or building including interest in land or building" and "(m) money or securities including interest in money or securities". Zero-rated supplies (s.187) include exported services and exported incorporeal property — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- Oyedele said in Apr 2025 that VAT and stamp duties would be exempted from property rent — [AllAfrica](https://allafrica.com/stories/202504250205.html); see also [ThisDay Feb 2026](https://www.thisdaylive.com/2026/02/16/nigeria-tax-act-2025-setting-the-record-straight-on-housing-vat-and-the-25-tax-claim)
- **Small-business VAT relief (NTAA s.22(4)–(8)).** The monthly VAT return duty "shall not apply to a small business". A small business may opt out of the exemption, "including registration, charging of tax on its taxable supplies and filing of returns", by written notice. Capital-asset disposals and sale of the business are excluded from the threshold computation. A business that stops being small must file monthly returns. "Small Business" is defined as turnover ≤₦100m and fixed assets ≤₦250m in the certified NTAA — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf); [Forvis Mazars](https://www.forvismazars.com/ng/en/services/tax/nigeria-s-tax-reform-in-focus/2025-nta-vat-key-business-changes). **[CONFLICT]** Taxjeje says the gazetted NTAA sets ₦50m — [Taxjeje](https://taxjeje.com/insights/vat-threshold-50-million-not-100-million). **[OLD]** The previous VAT threshold was ₦25m — [Techpoint](https://techpoint.africa/guide/nigerian-tax-reform-acts/)
- **VAT returns (NTAA s.22(1)).** Due on or before the 21st of the following month "whether or not an economic activity has taken place". An extension to file does not extend time to pay. Where NRS deploys technology, returns may be required in real time (s.22(10)) — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf); [PwC](https://taxsummaries.pwc.com/nigeria/corporate/tax-administration)
- **VAT invoices (NTA s.153).** Must be sequentially numbered and show the supplier's Tax ID, invoice number, supplier name and address, CAC number, date, purchaser name, gross amount, and VAT charged and rate. NRS may direct use of e-invoices on 30 days' notice — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- **Input VAT (s.156).** Now claimable on services and fixed assets used for taxable supplies, apportioned for mixed supplies, within 5 years — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf); [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/nigeria-tax-act-2025-has-been-signed-highlights)
- **Platforms and non-residents (NTA s.151).** Non-resident suppliers must register and charge VAT. If they do not, the Nigerian taxable recipient must withhold or self-account. NRS "may, by notice, appoint any person, including a non-resident supplier … to collect the VAT". Under s.151(5), where the appointed person "does not process payments … but receives commission relating to the supplies, the appointed person shall use the mechanism with which it collects its commission to collect the VAT". Under s.155, government bodies and NRS-appointed persons must withhold VAT and remit it by the 14th of the following month. NRS may direct recipients of VAT-free invoices to self-account — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- NRS Information Circular 2026/18 on VAT withholding (29 June 2026) reportedly replaced FIRS Circular 2023/14. It covers appointed agents, MDAs and non-resident suppliers, with remittance by the 14th (secondary sources only) — [Orbitax](https://orbitax.com/news/country/article/Nigeria-Revenue-Service-Issues-62783); [RegFollower](https://regfollower.com/nigeria-nrs-issues-new-guidelines-on-capital-gains-taxation-vat-refund-claims/)
- **Fiscalisation.** Under NTA s.158 and NTAA s.23, any person making taxable supplies must use the Electronic Fiscal System when NRS deploys it. **[PENDING]** NRS must issue the regulation and transition arrangement (s.23(3)–(4)). Failure to process a supply through the system costs ₦200,000 plus 100% of the tax due plus interest at the CBN MPR (NTAA s.104). Refusing NRS technology deployment costs ₦1m on the first day and ₦10,000 per day after (s.103) — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
- **E-invoicing rollout (NRS Feb 2026 schedule).**
  - Large taxpayers (over ₦5bn turnover): compliance deadline 31 July 2026, after an earlier 30 June date — [Guardian](https://guardian.ng/news/nrs-sets-july-31-e-invoicing-deadline/); [BusinessDay](https://businessday.ng/business-economy/article/june-30-e-invoicing-deadline-puts-nigerias-tax-reforms-to-test/)
  - Medium taxpayers (₦1bn–₦5bn): go-live 1 July 2026, enforcement from Jan 2027.
  - Emerging/small taxpayers (under ₦1bn): go-live 1 July 2027, enforcement from Jan 2028 — [Sovos](https://sovos.com/regulatory-updates/vat/nigeria-nrs-publishes-e-invoicing-timeline/); [VATupdate](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/02/28/nigeria-sets-e-invoicing-rollout-dates-for-medium-and-emerging-taxpayers-through-2028/)
  - **[CONFLICT]** Some older sources claimed e-invoicing would be mandatory for all VAT-registered businesses from 1 Jan 2026 or Nov 2025 — [Global VAT Compliance](https://www.globalvatcompliance.com/globalvatnews/nigeria-e-invoicing-rollout-key-updates-2026/)

### Inferences
- Rent collected from tenants for landlords is exempt from VAT. HomeAI's commission or service fee on that rent is a taxable service at 7.5%, unless HomeAI is a small business that has not opted in. Sales commissions, listing fees, subscriptions and escrow fees are likewise taxable. Escrowed money itself is exempt under "money or securities".
- Because HomeAI takes commissions on supplies it facilitates, NRS could appoint it under s.151(3)/(5) or s.155(1)(b) as a VAT collection agent. This matters mainly for VAT-able supplies on the platform (e.g., agent or service-provider fees), since rent is exempt.
- Under the ₦1bn turnover band, HomeAI's e-invoicing (MBS) go-live is 1 July 2027 if the published schedule holds. Building invoice data fields (Tax ID, sequential numbers) into the product now lowers later cost.
- Opting into VAT registration while small lets HomeAI recover input VAT (e.g., on cloud or professional services) but adds monthly filing duties.

### Gaps
- The text of NRS guidance on appointing digital platforms or marketplaces as VAT agents under the new law was not retrieved.
- The NRS e-invoicing primary notice was not retrieved (secondary reporting only).

## Q5. Withholding tax (deduction at source) on commissions, rent, professional and contractor payments

### Takeaway
The Deduction of Tax at Source (Withholding) Regulations 2024 (effective 1 July 2024) still supply the rates. No new NTA-specific WHT regulations were found. Rates: 5% on commission, consultancy, technical, management and professional fees to residents (10% non-residents); 10% on rent; 2% on supply of goods or services. Rates double where the payee has no Tax ID. Under NTAA s.107, deductions must be remitted by the 21st of the following month.

### Cited Findings
- 2024 Regulations rates: commission, consultancy, technical, management and professional fees 5% (residents) and 10% (non-residents, final tax). Rent, hire and lease 10%. Supply of goods and services generally 2% (residents). Dividends and interest 10% — [PwC highlights](https://pwc.com/ng/en/assets/pdf/highlights-of-the-deduction-at-source-regulations-2024.pdf); [KPMG gazetted regulations](https://assets.kpmg.com/content/dam/kpmg/ng/pdf/2024/10/Deduction%20of%20Tax%20at%20Source%20%28Withholding%29%20Regulations%202024_Gazetted.pdf); [Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/withholding-tax/1491814/an-overview-of-the-nigerian-withholding-tax-regulations-of-2024)
- The 2024 Regulations took effect 1 July 2024. A small company (turnover under ₦25m) need not deduct if the recipient has a valid TIN and the transaction is ₦2,000,000 or less. Payees without a TIN suffer double the rate on non-passive income — [Mondaq overview](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/withholding-tax/1525324/overview-of-the-deducting-tax-at-source-withholding-regulations-2024)
- Exempt transactions include "broker commissions retained from principal's monies, per industry norm", over-the-counter spot purchases with no prior contract, bank direct-debit fees, insurance premiums, and distinguishable out-of-pocket expenses — [Mondaq overview](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/withholding-tax/1525324/overview-of-the-deducting-tax-at-source-withholding-regulations-2024)
- **[OLD]** Remittance under the 2024 Regulations: FIRS by the 21st. State IRS: CGT by the 10th, other deductions by the 30th — [Mondaq overview](https://www.mondaq.com/nigeria/withholding-tax/1525324/overview-of-the-deducting-tax-at-source-withholding-regulations-2024)
- **New law.** Under NTAA s.28, every person obliged to deduct must render monthly returns. Under s.107, failure to remit by the "21st day of the month immediately succeeding the month in which the amount was deducted" costs the amount, plus a 10% per annum penalty, plus interest at the CBN MPR, with up to 3 years' imprisonment on conviction. Under s.105, failure to deduct costs 40% of the amount not deducted — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
- Adviser summary: WHT is remitted by the 21st of the following month, the annual WHT filing is due 31 March, and related-party payments are due by the earlier of month-end after payment or when the liability is recognised — [Tundeadisa](https://tundeadisa.com/media_insights/know-your-taxes-the-tax-reforms-and-your-statutory-filing-obligations-a-simplified-guide/)
- Under the certified NTAA's deduction-at-source provisions, a non-resident company providing technical, consulting, professional or management services to a labelled startup suffers a 5% deduction, which is its final tax. The regulations on deduction at source apply — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
- LIRS requires employers' annual returns to include payments to service providers, vendors and consultants with proof of taxes deducted and remitted. Monthly PAYE and WHT returns are mandatory — [TechEconomy](https://www.techeconomy.ng/lagos-employers-must-file-2025-tax-returns-by-january-31-lirs)

### Inferences
- **Agent commissions paid by HomeAI:** deduct 5% WHT (10% if the agent has no Tax ID). Remit to NRS if the agent is a company, or to the agent's state IRS if an individual, by the 21st. If HomeAI is itself under ₦25m turnover, payments of ₦2m or less to agents with a TIN are exempt under the 2024 Regulations.
- **Commission HomeAI retains from rent it collects:** this may fall within the "broker commissions retained from principal's monies" exemption, so landlords would not need to withhold on it. Needs confirmation.
- **Rent HomeAI pays on behalf of corporate tenants:** the 10% rent WHT obligation sits with the payer. The agency and escrow structure determines whether HomeAI or the tenant is the deductor. This needs legal structuring.
- **Corporate customers paying HomeAI fees:** expect 5% or 2% WHT deducted from HomeAI's invoices. HomeAI should collect WHT credit notes and receipts to offset its CIT.

### Gaps
- No NRS or Ministry regulations replacing the 2024 WHT Regulations under the NTA were found. Their continued validity rests on the NTA/NTAA savings and transitional provisions and was not verified textually.
- The exact NTAA section number for the 5% startup non-resident WHT was not captured.

## Q6. PAYE and personal income tax (Lagos IRS / FCT-IRS) — new bands

### Takeaway
From 2026 the PIT bands are: 0% on the first ₦800k, 15%, 18%, 21%, 23%, and 25% above ₦50m. There is a new rent relief of 20% of rent up to ₦500k, and minimum-wage earners are exempt. Remittance is reportedly due by the 10th, and the employer's annual return by 31 January.

## Q7. Stamp duties: tenancy agreements and leases, electronic receipts, responsibility, electronic documents

### Takeaway
Stamp duty is now in NTA Chapter 5 and the Eighth/Ninth Schedules (the Stamp Duties Act is repealed). Leases with annual value under ₦10m, or 10 times the annual minimum wage if higher, are not dutiable (s.135). Above that, the lessee pays 0.78% for leases up to 7 years and reportedly 3% for longer leases. Instruments must be stamped within 30 days. Electronic receipts and transfers of ₦10,000 or more attract ₦50 fixed duty borne by the transferor. Duty can be denoted electronically.

### Cited Findings
- NTA s.124 imposes duty on instruments executed in Nigeria, or executed abroad but relating to Nigerian property or matters, at Schedule rates. Under s.125, duty may be denoted by tax stamps, die, "electronic or digital tagging", "electronic receipt", certificate, or other means the authority sets — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- NTA s.126: dutiable instruments must be stamped "not later than 30 days after its execution". The transferee of an interest in real property, or the beneficiary of a paid service, is responsible for the duty. Under s.127, unstamped instruments are inadmissible in civil or arbitral proceedings (but usable in criminal proceedings) — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- NTA s.135 (Leases): agreements for leases are dutiable as if actual leases, "provided that lease agreements for which the annual value is less than N10,000,000 or 10 times the annual minimum wage, whichever is higher, shall not be chargeable with any duty" — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf). **[CONFLICT]** Oyedele was quoted describing the exemption as rents below ₦10m a month, against the statutory "annual value" — [Estate Intel](https://estateintel.com/insights/the-nigerian-tax-act-2025-implications-for-the-real-estate-sector)
- Schedule item 22, "Demise (see Lease)", is ad valorem, payable by the lessee, at "Up to 7 years = 0.78%". The above-7-years rate is cut off in the gazette PDF extracted — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf). EY reports leases under 7 years at 0.78% and over 7 years at 3%, low-rent leases exempt, property transactions under ₦10m exempt, and stamping within 30 days — [EY](https://www.ey.com/en_gl/technical/tax-alerts/nigeria-tax-act-2025-has-been-signed-highlights). Estate Intel gives 0.78% under 7 years and 3% for 8–21 years — [Estate Intel](https://estateintel.com/insights/the-nigerian-tax-act-2025-implications-for-the-real-estate-sector)
- **[OLD]** FIRS-era (2020) tenancy rates were 0.78% (under 7 years), 3% (7–21 years) and 6% (over 21 years), paid by tenants — [Estate Intel 2020](https://estateintel.com/insights/firs-introduces-stamp-duty-on-rent-c-of-o-among-others)
- Schedule item 48: "Electronic receipt or Transfer of N10,000 upwards", fixed duty ₦50, payable by the transferor. Money moved between the same owner's accounts in the same bank is exempt. Item 47: receipts of ₦10,000 or more, ₦50. Under s.185(i), electronic transfers or receipts below ₦10,000, salary transfers and intra-bank self-transfers are exempt — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- Conveyance on sale and transfers of real property (s.131; item 17) are 1.5%, payable by the transferee. Exchanges of real property above ₦1m or the annual minimum wage are dutiable as conveyances (s.134). Under s.139, property appraisals or valuations for a fee are dutiable and accounted for by the appraiser — [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf)
- Under NTAA s.110, failing to stamp costs 10% of the unpaid duty plus interest at the CBN MPR — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
- **[CONFLICT] Administering authority.** NTAA s.3(1)(b) gives NRS power over stamp duties, and s.87 preserves state power over stamp duties for individuals — [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf). An academic article claims NRS has exclusive power over all stamp duties — [UNIZIK journal](https://journals.unizik.edu.ng/naujppl/article/download/8061/6797/18414)

### Inferences
- Most Nigerian residential tenancies on HomeAI (annual rent under ₦10m) are now outside stamp duty. Leases of ₦10m+ a year attract 0.78% (7 years or less), payable by the tenant/lessee within 30 days.
- HomeAI is not the statutory payer, but if it generates e-tenancy agreements it should build in a stamping workflow or advice so documents stay admissible in disputes. Electronic denotation is expressly allowed.
- The ₦50 electronic-transfer duty is borne by the transferor and is typically collected by banks. Rent transfers via HomeAI's payment rails will attract it per transfer of ₦10,000 or more, unless the transfer is between the same owner's accounts at the same bank.

### Gaps
- The exact above-7-year lease rate and any 21-year tier in the Eighth Schedule are unconfirmed (text cut off).
- How stamp duty collection on electronic receipts by banks and payment processors is mechanised under the NTA (any 2026 NRS or CBN circular) was not found.

## Q8. Capital gains on property transactions (relevance to the platform)

### Takeaway

## Q10. Penalties and interest; tax incentives for startups

### Takeaway
The NTAA sets fixed administrative penalties and interest at the CBN MPR plus spread:
- failure to register: ₦50k, then ₦25k/month
- failure to file: ₦100k, then ₦50k/month
- late payment: 10% plus interest
- failure to deduct: 40%
- failure to remit deductions: 10% p.a. plus interest
- fiscalisation failure: ₦200k plus 100% of tax plus interest

For incentives, the small-company 0% (CIT, CGT, development levy) is the main relief for HomeAI. The Startup Act tax provisions in s.25(2)–(4) and s.29(3) were deleted. Pioneer status was replaced by the Economic Development Tax Incentive (EDTI, a 5% annual credit on qualifying capex), which is unlikely to suit an asset-light startup.

### Cited Findings

  - s.100 failure to register: ₦50,000 first month, ₦25,000 each later month; awarding a contract to an unregistered person, ₦5m.

  - Sources: [NTA gazette copy](https://irs.gm.gov.ng/docs/national/NIGERIA_TAX_ACT_2025.pdf); [NTAA certified copy](https://media.premiumtimesng.com/wp-content/files/2026/01/Approved-Copy-to-Print-NIGERIA-TAX-ADMINISTRATION-ACT-2025.pdf.pdf)
