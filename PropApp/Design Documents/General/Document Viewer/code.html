This Lease Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into this 1st day of December, 2023, by and between:

Landlord

Oakwood Management Group

Mark Anderson

Current Address: 1243 Oakwood Ave

1. The Premises

Landlord agrees to lease to Tenant, and Tenant agrees to lease from Landlord, the single-family residence located at 1243 Oakwood Avenue, Austin, TX 78704 (the "Premises"). The Premises shall be used strictly for residential purposes.

2. Lease Term

The initial term of this Lease shall begin on December 1, 2023 and end on November 30, 2024. Tenant shall vacate the Premises upon termination of the Agreement, unless: (i) the Landlord and Tenant have agreed in writing to extend this Agreement or sign a new lease agreement; (ii) the Landlord accepts Rent from the Tenant (other than past due Rent), in which case a month-to-month tenancy shall be created.

3. Rent & Payments

Tenant shall pay to Landlord the sum of $4,800.00 per month as Rent for the Term of the Agreement. The Rent shall be due on the first day of each month.
