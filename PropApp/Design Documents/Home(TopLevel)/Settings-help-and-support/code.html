How do I verify my identity as a Landlord? expand_more
To verify your identity, go to your Landlord Dashboard and navigate to the Verification tab. You will need to upload a valid government-issued ID and a proof of property ownership. Our AI system will review your documents within 24 hours.
Can I change my account type from Tenant to Agent? expand_more
Yes, PropertyApp supports multiple personas on a single account. You don't need to change your type; simply add the Agent persona by visiting the "Manage Persona Profiles" section in your Account Settings.
What payment methods are accepted? expand_more
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), direct bank transfers, and digital wallets like PayPal. You can manage your payment methods in the Payments section of your Global Settings.
